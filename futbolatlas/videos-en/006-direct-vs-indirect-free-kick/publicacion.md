## Sources and caveats

Everything comes from [Direct vs indirect free kick](https://futbolatlas.app/k/direct-vs-indirect-free-kick), [Direct free kick](https://futbolatlas.app/k/direct-free-kick) and [Indirect free kick](https://futbolatlas.app/k/indirect-free-kick), which cite Laws 12 and 13 of The IFAB's Laws of the Game 2026/27 (verified on the site on 8 August 2026).

| In the video | On the site |
|---|---|
| If the referee raises an arm, that free kick cannot go straight in | "an indirect free kick comes with a raised arm, held up until the ball is touched by another player or goes out of play" |
| A direct free kick can go straight into the net. An indirect one needs another player to touch the ball | "A direct free kick can be scored by kicking the ball straight into the opponents' goal. An indirect free kick only produces a goal if another player — of either team — touches the ball" |
| If an indirect kick flies in untouched, it is a goal kick | "If an indirect kick flies in untouched, the restart is a goal kick, not a goal" |
| Direct: contact (trips, pushes, holding) plus handball | "charging, jumping at, kicking, pushing, striking, tackling, tripping, holding and impeding with contact… plus handball" |
| Indirect: dangerous play, offside or the back-pass to the keeper | "dangerous play, impeding without contact, dissent, offside, and the goalkeeper handling offences (holding too long, back-pass, throw-in from a team-mate)" |
| A direct free kick offence inside the area is a penalty. An indirect one stays an indirect free kick | "a direct free kick offence by the defending team inside its own penalty area becomes a penalty kick. An indirect free kick offence in the same place stays an indirect free kick" |

- **Simplifications:**
  - Contact offences give a direct free kick when careless, reckless or with excessive force; the video just says "contact".
  - The offence lists are not complete (biting, spitting, dissent and impeding without contact are left out, among others).
  - It is only a penalty when the defending team commits the offence in its own area.
  - An indirect free kick offence inside the goal area is taken from the goal area line; the video leaves that out.
  - The "other player" who touches the ball can be from either team.
