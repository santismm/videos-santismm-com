## Sources and caveats

Everything comes from [DOGSO](https://futbolatlas.app/k/dogso) and [Law 12 - Fouls and Misconduct](https://futbolatlas.app/k/law-12-fouls-and-misconduct), which cite Law 12 of the Laws of the Game 2026/27 and the IFAB 2026/27 law changes (verified on the site on 8 August 2026).

| In the video | On the site |
|---|---|
| Law 12 gives a red card for denying a goal or an obvious goal-scoring opportunity; a deliberate handball counts too | "Denying the opposing team a goal or an obvious goal-scoring opportunity, including by a deliberate handball offence, is a sending-off offence under Law 12" |
| From this edition, the referee weighs where the defenders and attackers are, and how many | "the DOGSO considerations now include the location and number of defenders and attackers" |
| Inside the box, if it's a penalty and the defender was trying to play the ball, it's only a yellow | "the offender is cautioned rather than sent off if the offence was an attempt to play the ball or a challenge for the ball" |
| Holding, pulling, pushing, or no chance of playing the ball: red card and penalty (on screen, also "handball") | "in all other circumstances (holding, pulling, pushing, no possibility to play the ball, or handball) the offender is sent off" |
| If the referee plays advantage and the move ends in a goal: since this year, no card | "when the referee plays advantage for a DOGSO offence and the non-offending team scores as a result, there is no disciplinary sanction" |

- **Our own inference:** the site does not literally say that "last man" is absent from the Law. The hook rests on the category the site defines (denying an obvious goal-scoring opportunity) and on the fact that the location and number of defenders and attackers are weighed. Worth validating.
- **Simplifications:**
  - The site does not list every consideration for deciding whether a chance is "obvious"; the video only covers the ones it records.
  - "Since this year" and "this edition" mean the 2026/27 edition of the Laws.
  - The yellow card in the box also applies to a challenge for the ball, not only an attempt to play it.
