## Description

By 1521 Spain knew how to cross the Pacific toward Asia. Getting back was another matter: in the tropics the trade winds blow west. In 1565 Andrés de Urdaneta solved it by climbing to the 39th parallel, where the wind and the Kuroshio current push toward America. That route, the tornaviaje, was sailed by the Manila Galleon for two and a half centuries.
