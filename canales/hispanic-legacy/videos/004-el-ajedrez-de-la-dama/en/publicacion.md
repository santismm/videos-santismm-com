## Description

In medieval chess the piece we now call the queen moved just one square diagonally. In the late fifteenth century the queen and the bishop gained their long moves. The first printed book on that new game was written by Lucena, a student at Salamanca, in 1497 at the latest; he called it “de la Dama”, the queen’s chess. In Italy it was called “alla rabiosa”.

Caveat: nobody knows who first moved the queen that way, or where. Chess is not a Spanish invention: it reached Iberia from the Islamic world.
