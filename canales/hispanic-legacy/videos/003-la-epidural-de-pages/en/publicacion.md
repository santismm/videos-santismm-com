## Description

In 1921 the military surgeon Fidel Pagés published “Anestesia metamérica” in the Revista Española de Cirugía: the first description of the epidural in humans, with anatomy, technique, cases and complications. He died two years later, aged 37. In the 1930s the Italian Dogliotti popularized his own version without crediting him, until the Argentinian Alberto Gutiérrez insisted and Dogliotti acknowledged him in 1939.

Caveat: what is attributed to Pagés is the first description. The loss-of-resistance method taught today is Dogliotti’s own contribution.
