## Description

In 1801 Andrés Manuel del Río, professor of mineralogy in Mexico City, found a new element in a lead mineral. He sent a sample to Paris, where chemists wrongly concluded it was a chromium mineral. Thirty years later the Swede Sefström rediscovered it and named it vanadium. Today the Royal Society of Chemistry credits the first discovery to Del Río.
