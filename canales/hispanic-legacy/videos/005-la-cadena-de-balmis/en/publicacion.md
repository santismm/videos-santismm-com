No fridges: the vaccine that crossed the ocean arm to arm #history

## Description

In 1803 there was no way to preserve a vaccine on an ocean crossing. The Balmis expedition kept it alive by passing it arm to arm among 22 boys from the Corunna foundling home, cared for by Isabel Zendal. They vaccinated in the Canaries, Puerto Rico, Venezuela, Cuba, Mexico and the Philippines, and carried the vaccine on to China, leaving vaccination boards behind. Medical historians call it the first international vaccination campaign.
