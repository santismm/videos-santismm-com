## Title (YouTube Shorts)

“The largest empire in history” is about maps, not governments #history

## Description

Empire-size figures come from historical atlases: they measure each empire’s coloured patch. That way the British Empire reaches about 35 million km² around 1920 and the Spanish 13–14 million around 1780. But one colour can hide colonies, protectorates and mere claims. And the alternative “effective control” figures are not reproducible either: they don’t say which year they measure, or by what rules.
