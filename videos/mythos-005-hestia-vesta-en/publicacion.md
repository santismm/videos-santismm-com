Two things can share a function and not a single story.

Hestia and Vesta are usually presented as "the same goddess". By function, they are: hearth and virginity. But in the Greek texts Hestia has almost no narrative and no priests. Vesta, in Rome, has a state-paid institution (Livy), a fire carried from Troy (Virgil) and a Vestal as Romulus's mother.

Equivalent doesn't mean identical. Before you say "it's the same thing", ask which text says so.
