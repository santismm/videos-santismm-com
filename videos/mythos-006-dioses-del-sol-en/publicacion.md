**Nuance the video keeps:** the corpus does not say that nobody in antiquity identified Apollo with the sun. It says that **in Homer, Hesiod and the Hymns** he is not the sun, and that the blend shows up in the Latin poets ("the Apollo-as-sun idea is Latin"). Hence "in Homer and the Hymns" and "that is where they blend".
