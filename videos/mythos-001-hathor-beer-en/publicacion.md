How many "facts" we repeat are really labels someone added later?

Hathor is almost everywhere described as "the Egyptian goddess of love". But in the Egyptian text of the Destruction of Mankind (tomb of Seti I) she is the Eye of Ra: she nearly wipes out humanity, and Ra only stops her by getting her drunk on 7,000 jars of beer dyed red.

And the love part? It comes from the Greek identification with Aphrodite.

The lesson travels well beyond mythology: before repeating something, ask which source says so.
