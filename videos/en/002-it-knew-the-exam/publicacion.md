## Sources and caveats

- **Base article:** [The Stopwatch and the Exam](https://articles.santismm.com/the-stopwatch-and-the-exam/) ([Spanish version](https://articles.santismm.com/el-cronometro-y-el-examen/)).
- **Konwinski Prize (K Prize):** it is built only from GitHub issues filed after the training cutoff. The winner scored 7.5%.
  - The article: "The winning entry scored 7.5%, against the roughly 75% comparable systems report" on SWE-bench Verified.
  - That is why the video says "comparable systems" and "the same kind of exam", not "the same model".
- **"Up to 45%":** the article cites surveys that find "test material leaking into training data at rates reported as high as 45%". It is a maximum, not an average.
- **Remedies:** saturated → a harder exam; contaminated → an exam the model has never seen. In the article's words: "The remedy for saturation is a harder benchmark. The remedy for contamination is a benchmark the model has never seen."
- **"Model X: 96%":** a generic example, not a real score attributed to anyone.
