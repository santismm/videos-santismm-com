## Sources and caveats

Source article: [The agentic enterprise needs an immune system](https://articles.santismm.com/the-agentic-enterprise-needs-an-immune-system/) ([Spanish version](https://articles.santismm.com/la-empresa-agentica-necesita-un-sistema-inmunitario/)), section "Four ways to become ill". The article says: "Agentic failures do not all share one cause."

**The four classes:**
1. **Input** (instructions hidden in data): "An email, website, retrieved document, or tool output contains instructions the agent mistakes for authority."
2. **Permission** (excessive agency): "The agent has more functions, privileges, or autonomy than it needs. The model may fail, but the permission architecture determines the blast radius."
3. **Objective** (goal drift): "closing unresolved cases, or meeting a metric by hiding exceptions".
4. **Network** (cross-component propagation): "A contaminated result passes to another agent, enters memory, and later returns as apparently trusted context." It adds: "Trust should not be inherited merely because another agent sent the message."

**Digital autoimmunity** (Figure 5 caption): "an overreaction can damage healthy services while distracting from the real attack."

**The cycle** (Figure 2 caption): "From perimeter to immune cycle: validate, authorize, observe, contain, and learn."

**Made-up elements in the video:** the tickets (#1041–#1044), the keys and the services are illustrative examples. The article gives no figures for this section, so the video uses none.
